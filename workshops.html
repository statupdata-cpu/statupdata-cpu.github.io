Workshop 2 — SPSS Hands-on: Basics → Intermediate

Overview: A 3-day hands-on workshop introducing SPSS for statistical analysis. Topics include data import, cleaning, descriptive statistics, hypothesis testing, regression, and reporting. Sessions are practical, using real datasets and step-by-step demonstrations for beginners and intermediate learners. (21–23 May 2026, 7:00–9:00 PM). By the end, participants will confidently prepare data, run essential tests, interpret results, and generate research-ready reports.

Course structure

  • Day 1 (Basic): Data handling, import, cleaning, descriptive statistics and basic charts.
  • Day 2 (Intermediate): Hypothesis testing (t-tests, chi-square, correlation) and interpretation.
  • Day 3 (Advanced): Regression (linear, logistic basics), reporting outputs and export of results.

Workshop 3 — Python for Data Analysis, Visualization & Statistical Modeling

Overview: A 4-day intensive online workshop designed to provide structured, hands-on training in Python for data analysis, statistical inference, visualization, regression modeling, classification, and time series forecasting. Participants will work with real datasets and develop practical analytical skills applicable to research and industry workflows.

Course structure (27 April – 30 April)

  • Day 1: Python basics, NumPy & Pandas, data cleaning & exploratory data analysis.
  • Day 2: Statistical inference — probability distributions, confidence intervals, hypothesis testing & data visualization.
  • Day 3: Regression modeling (linear & multiple), classification techniques & model evaluation.
  • Day 4: Time series analysis & forecasting (ARIMA basics) with mini project.
